# KisanVerify Multilingual Terminology Glossary
=================================================
Approved agricultural, financial, and policy terminology for KisanVerify.
Used as the authoritative reference across English, Hindi, and regional language translations.

## Core Philosophical & Tone Principles
- **Neutral Non-Accusatory Wording**:
  - English: *"may need review"* | *"difference"* | *"unexplained amount"*
  - Hindi: *"समीक्षा आवश्यक हो सकती है"* | *"अंतर"* | *"अव्याख्यायित राशि"*
  - *Never use*: "fraud", "scam", "theft", "illegal", "embezzlement", "cheating".
- **Non-Guarantee of Benefits**:
  - English: *"may be eligible"* | *"indicative eligibility"*
  - Hindi: *"संभावित पात्र"* | *"सांकेतिक पात्रता"*
  - *Never use*: "guaranteed approval", "100% promised".
- **Scheme Names & Official Authorities**:
  - Scheme names like "PM-KISAN", "PMFBY", "KCC", "e-NAM" are statutory brand names and must not be translated into synthetic words.
  - Official URLs and portal references remain untampered.
